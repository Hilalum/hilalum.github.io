# Privacy Policy for AvatarCost

**Last Updated: September 22, 2026**

## Introduction

Thank you for using **AvatarCost** ("the App"). AvatarCost is an iOS application that looks up a public avatar by username, lists the items it is wearing, and estimates what those items would cost today.

We value your privacy. AvatarCost has no account system and never asks you to log in to anything. It stores your lookup history on your device only. It does make outbound network requests — to the source platform's public web API in order to perform the lookup you asked for, to our analytics service for privacy-minimized product events, and to Google AdMob only when you choose to watch an optional rewarded video. These are described below.

## Data Collection and Use

1. **No account, no login**: The App has no sign-up, no sign-in, and no user account. It never asks for a password or an authentication token for any service.

2. **Usernames you look up**: When you search for an avatar, the username you type is sent to the **source platform's public web API** so it can return that avatar's public profile, worn items, and prices. This is the core function of the App and cannot happen without that request. The username is sent to that platform only. It is **never** sent to our analytics service.

3. **Your own username (optional)**: You may optionally tell the App your own username so that every avatar you check can be compared against yours. This value is stored **only on your device**, is used only to perform the same public lookup described above, and can be changed or removed at any time in Settings. It is never sent to our analytics service.

4. **Photo Library Access**: The App requests **add-only** photo access, and only at the moment you choose to save a share card. Add-only access lets the App write one image you asked it to save; it does **not** give the App the ability to read, browse, or list your photo library. Sharing a card or video through the iOS share sheet does not require this permission at all.

5. **Local Storage**: Your lookup history, saved outfits, cached item metadata, usage counters, and purchase entitlement are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App removes them.

6. **Product analytics**: AvatarCost sends privacy-minimized product interaction events to a developer-operated analytics service. These events help us understand whether search, the free-lookup limit, rewarded-ad choices, sharing, and the lifetime purchase flow work as intended. An event may include the event name, time, app version, build number, platform, locale, a short-lived funnel identifier, and allowlisted context such as the screen or result. We do **not** send the username you searched for, your own stored username, any game user ID or item ID, the raw text you typed, avatar values, names, email addresses, account identifiers, advertising identifiers, persistent device identifiers, images, or payment information. Analytics events are not linked to your identity and are not used to track you across apps or websites.

## Advertising

AvatarCost uses **Google AdMob** to display **optional rewarded video ads**. An ad appears only when you have used your free daily lookup and you choose to watch one in order to unlock an additional lookup. There are no banner ads and no interstitial/pop-up ads.

To deliver, measure, secure, and improve these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as IP address or IP-derived coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash logs, and performance data. Google may process this information for third-party advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and app functionality, subject to your consent choices and applicable law.

AvatarCost requests **non-personalized ads** and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized ads are not selected using your past behavior, but Google may still use identifiers or local storage for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, fraud prevention, and ad delivery. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before ad requests are enabled. AvatarCost itself does not combine analytics events with third-party data and does not use them to track you across other apps or websites.

For details on how Google handles this data, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## In-App Purchases

AvatarCost offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Lifetime Pro") that removes the daily lookup limit and the rewarded-ad gate. Payment is processed securely through Apple's App Store via your Apple ID.

* We do not have access to your full payment information. Apple provides us with limited transaction data (such as purchase confirmation) necessary to deliver the purchased features.

## Third-Party Services

* **Source platform (public web API)**: Receives the username you choose to look up, in order to return that avatar's public data. We send nothing else to it and we do not create or use an account with it. See the [source platform's privacy policy](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004630823-Roblox-Privacy-and-Cookie-Policy). AvatarCost is an independent app and is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by that platform or its publisher.
* **Google AdMob**: Serves the optional rewarded video ads described in the Advertising section. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Hilalum Analytics**: Receives the privacy-minimized product interaction events described above. It does not receive searched usernames, game identifiers, avatar values, images, or free-form user content.
* **Apple App Store**: In-app purchases are processed by Apple. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Public Data Only

AvatarCost reads only data that the source platform already publishes publicly for any visitor, without logging in. It cannot see private inventories, private accounts, messages, friends lists, balances, or purchase history. Prices shown are the platform's own published prices; items with no published price are listed but deliberately excluded from any total rather than estimated.

## Data Security

* Your history, saved outfits, and settings are stored locally on your device.
* All network requests are made over HTTPS.
* Aside from the lookups you request and the analytics events and ad requests described above, the App does not transmit your data over the internet.

## Children's Privacy

This App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Rewarded ads are optional and are requested as non-personalized ads.

## Your Choices

* **Stop sharing your own username**: remove it in Settings at any time.
* **Clear your history**: delete individual entries in the App, or delete the App to remove all local data.
* **Ad consent**: where a consent form is presented, you can change your choice from the privacy options it provides.
* **Remove ads entirely**: purchase Lifetime Pro.
