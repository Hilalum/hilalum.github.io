RadioFax Support

Need help with RadioFax? Please contact support through the project issue page:

https://github.com/Hilalum/RadioFax/issues

Helpful details to include

  • Device and OS version
  • Whether you used microphone input or an audio file
  • WEFAX/NOAA mode, LPM, and IOC if known
  • A short description of what went wrong

Privacy information is available in the RadioFax Privacy Policy.
