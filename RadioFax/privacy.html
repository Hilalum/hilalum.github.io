RadioFax Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

RadioFax is designed to decode weather fax audio on your device. The app does not collect personal data, does not track you, and does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data processed on device

RadioFax may process audio that you provide through the microphone or a selected audio file in order to decode weather fax images. Decoded charts and app history are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Permissions

  • Microphone access is used only to receive weather fax audio signals.
  • Photo library add access is used only when you choose to save decoded weather fax images.
  • File access is used only for files you select.

Data collection

RadioFax does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app does not operate tracking domains.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, visit the RadioFax support page.
